Bullpen

Drag a reliever to another role, or onto a rotation spot to make him a starter (that starter takes his role). "Use in" defaults to his role: closer and setup in high leverage, middle in medium, long man for long relief. A specialist comes in for same-side batters. When the starter comes out, the sim reads the inning, score and bases and brings in the reliever whose setting fits; a closer isn't required.
